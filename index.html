How to Use This Guide

Statewide races (Governor, Lt. Governor, etc.) are on everyone's ballot regardless of where you live.
Local races (Congress, Assembly, State Senate, school boards) vary by your address. Look up your ballot at vote.ca.gov to see exactly what's on yours.
Judicial races are retention votes — you vote YES or NO on keeping each judge. They're nonpartisan.
Tap any race to expand it and read the candidates. Tap again to collapse. Use the Next → button at the bottom of each card to move through the ballot in order.
Election Day: November 3, 2026 · Polls open 7am–8pm · Or vote early by mail!

⚖️ Judicial — Retention Votes

These are retention votes, not contested elections. You vote YES to keep each justice, or NO to remove them. All were appointed by Democratic governors and have not drawn any organized opposition.

📋 Propositions & Measures

Prop 1 — Housing Bonds
Borrow $11.25 billion for affordable housing programs
▼
Would allow the state to sell $11.25 billion in bonds to fund affordable housing — $1.25B for veteran housing, the rest for rental homes, homeownership programs, farmworker housing, affordable student housing, tribal housing and more. Expected to help fund up to 40,000 multifamily rental units and help 40,000 households buy a home.

Context: Would replenish funding for several successful state programs that have exhausted their resources. Placed on the ballot by the Legislature.

✅ Supporters Say
California needs more money for housing to help the state's 180,000+ homeless residents, those who can't afford rent, and those who can't afford to purchase a home.

Supported by: California Democratic Party, Gov. Gavin Newsom, The California Apartment Association
❌ Opponents Say
Some Republican legislators criticized the bill for using veterans to drum up support while doing little to serve them. Also questioned the wisdom of borrowing more money without addressing underlying reasons housing is so expensive to build.

Opposed by: Some Republican State Lawmakers, including Tony Strickland and Carl DeMaio

Prop 2 — Rainy Day Fund

▼
